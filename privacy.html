Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-29

FolioTCG is a card-collection app made by an independent developer. The short version: your data stays with you. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no ads — and we run no servers of our own.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your collection — the cards you record, their prices, binders, decks, want lists, and preferences — is stored in a database on your device.
  • Card photos you take with the scanner are stored on your device and never uploaded by the app.
  • Backups: the app writes daily snapshots of your collection on your device, and copies the most recent snapshots to your own iCloud account's private container for this app, if you are signed in to iCloud. Those snapshots are yours: we cannot see, access, or receive them. Your device's own iCloud/computer backup may also include the app's data, under Apple's terms, like any app.

What leaves your device

  • Card lookups: to show card names, images, and prices, the app requests public card data from Scryfall (scryfall.com), the way a browser fetches a public web page. These requests identify cards, not you; the app attaches no account, name, or advertising identifier. Your IP address is visible to Scryfall as with any internet request; see Scryfall's own privacy policy for how they handle server logs.
  • Nothing else. No telemetry, no crash reporting, no analytics events, no advertising SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to see your collection, your photos, or your usage.

Sharing and selling

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing to share or sell, and we don't.

Children

FolioTCG does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — it collects none at all.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its on-device data is gone. Snapshots in your iCloud container can be removed by deleting the app's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Since the app collects nothing, changes would only ever describe new on-device or your-own-iCloud behavior.

Contact

Questions: see the support page, or email the address listed on the App Store product page.
