Support

The collection tracker that keeps your cards, prices, and backups in your own hands. No account needed — every feature works from first launch.

Common questions

How do I get my collection in from another app?
Collection tab → ⋯ → Import. Pick the CSV or decklist file your other tracker exported, or paste a decklist. The app reads what it was given and shows a receipt of exactly what landed — cards you own can go to a binder, a deck, a collection, or stay unfiled.
The scanner picked the wrong printing.
Tap the printing shown on the green receipt right after the scan — every printing of that card appears with pictures; tap the right one and the card is corrected in place. Scanning a whole box from one set? Tap Lock set on the camera so every scan resolves in that set first.
Where are my backups?
Settings → Backup. A snapshot of your whole collection is saved on your phone automatically every day, and the latest snapshots are also copied to your own iCloud when you're signed in. Restoring always shows what will be replaced and asks you to type RESTORE before it touches anything.
I got a new phone. How do I get my collection back?
Install FolioTCG, open Settings → Backup, and your iCloud snapshot appears in the restore list (marked ☁️). Restore it through the same confirmation. Your phone's own iCloud backup also restores the app's data when you restore the whole phone.
Do prices update?
Yes — prices come from Scryfall's public database and refresh regularly while you use the app. Each card also remembers what you paid (from your import or your entry), and shows change in dollars and percent.
Does the app upload my photos or my collection?
No. Card photos and your collection stay on your device; the only things the app ever writes outside your phone are your own backup snapshots, into your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
